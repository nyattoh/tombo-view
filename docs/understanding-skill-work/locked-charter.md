# Understanding-oriented TOMBO skill: locked goal charter

Date: 2026-10-20 JST. Parent: intake/coordinator. Goal: understanding-skill-20261020.

## Intended outcome

Make the repository's reusable tombo-view skill usable in ordinary development and conversation as sustained attention to the agent's own understanding. During an activated task it revisits what is understood, unsupported, uncertain, contradictory or overlooked as relevant new information arrives, and selects proceed, check, ask, reframe or blocked. Asking the user is one possible action, not the organising purpose. It uses observations and evidence rather than self-reported confidence, keeps work moving proportionately and updates its understanding after checks, corrections and human answers. Meaningful reconsideration must not be replaced by a ceremonial checklist or repeated questioning.
