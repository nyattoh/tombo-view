# Neighbourhood reading group: first-meeting formats

Synthetic, undated planning note for eight attendees. The room is available for 45 minutes.

- Open round: allow each attendee up to 2 minutes to share a thought, then leave the remaining time for open discussion. No materials are needed.
- Prompt-card activity: use three prompt cards in small groups. The activity outline reserves 35 minutes, including the introduction. Preparing the cards is estimated at about 10 minutes; this is a planning estimate, not a measured duration.
